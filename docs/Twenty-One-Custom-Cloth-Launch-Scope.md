# Twenty One Custom Cloth

## Executive Summary

Twenty One Custom Cloth will move forward with the approved **Sovereign** identity, refined into a **black-and-white luxury menswear system**. The goal is to create a cohesive digital presence that turns qualified interest into private fitting appointments. The launch will include a mobile-first website, appointment-request form, rebuilt Instagram and Facebook business profiles, launch content, and Meta advertising setup and management.

The brand will be positioned around **presence, precision, and private service**. Black-and-white imagery, disciplined typography, considered tailoring details, and appointment-led calls to action will make the business feel established, masculine, and premium without relying on decorative color or generic fashion messaging.

> **Primary launch objective:** generate qualified private-consultation requests for custom suits, tuxedos, jackets, and wardrobe planning.

### Website Content Needed From the Client

The website cannot accurately represent the business without final operational details. The client will provide or approve the business phone number, email address, business address or service area, consultation availability, service list, starting-price language if desired, biographies, client testimonials, garment imagery, and any legal business information required for the footer or privacy policy.

If professional photography is not available, the initial site can launch with a curated set of founder-provided images and premium licensed imagery. A dedicated menswear photo shoot is strongly recommended as a Phase 2 addition because original fitting and garment imagery will materially improve trust and ad performance.

## Recommended Launch Investment

The project should begin with two separate pools of funds: **implementation fees** for strategy, design, website development, and account setup; and **ad spend** paid directly to Meta for media placement. Keeping these separate makes the proposal transparent and protects the advertising budget from being consumed by production work.

| Investment category | Recommended structure | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery and project deposit | **50% of the agreed Phase 1 and Phase 2 project fee, due before work begins** | Reserves production time and funds the brand finalization, website planning, design, setup, and initial social work |
| Remaining project balance | **50% due before website launch and handoff** | Covers final revisions, website launch, content delivery, and connected assets |
| Initial Meta ad spend | **Recommended minimum: $1,000 for the first 30 days**, paid directly to Meta | Creates enough budget to test audience, creative, and lead quality in a local luxury-service market |
| Meta advertising management | A separate monthly management fee, agreed before campaign launch | Covers campaign creation, monitoring, optimization, reporting, and creative iteration |
| Optional content production | Quoted separately after choosing a photographer, videographer, wardrobe, locations, models, and deliverable count | Produces original premium imagery and video for the website and ads |
| Optional software costs | Domain, website hosting, scheduling software, email service, CRM, and any paid stock licenses billed at cost or selected by client | Keeps the operating stack connected and professional |

> **Suggested payment request to send now:** “To begin the black-and-white brand finalization, website build, social rebuild, and advertising foundation, I require a **50% project deposit** plus an initial **$1,000 Meta advertising budget**. The advertising budget is paid directly to Meta and is separate from my build and management fees.”

The exact project fee and monthly management fee should be inserted before this scope is sent. Do not commit to an advertising budget, turnaround time, or number of leads that cannot be supported by the client’s local market, creative assets, and availability.

The client must also designate who receives and follows up on leads, ideally within one business day.

The client will approve final logo use, website content, photos, service descriptions, pricing claims, ad creative, and advertising targeting before launch. Ad spend will be paid by the client directly to Meta. Advertising activity is subject to Meta’s platform policies and review process.
